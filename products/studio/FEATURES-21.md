# Toolshape Studio — first 21 feature families

The list is deliberately outcome-oriented. Each feature includes professional human controls and semantic agent operations.

## 1. Unified project and workspace

One project holds design pages, video scenes, assets, styles, transcripts, versions, outputs, and jobs. Workspaces rearrange the same objects for Create, Video, Review, and Automate.

## 2. Asset library and import

Images, video, audio, fonts, SVG/vector, data tables, PDFs/presentations where supported, project packages, and licensed/provider assets. Originals are immutable; proxies and thumbnails are derived artifacts.

## 3. Layered design canvas

Frames/artboards, groups, layers, ordering, visibility, lock, transforms, guides, snapping, grids, safe areas, multi-select, and responsive preview.

## 4. Text and typography

Live text, font management/licensing, styles, hierarchy, line/paragraph controls, lists, text boxes, overflow diagnostics, path/text effects later, and language-aware typography.

## 5. Shapes, vectors, drawing, and masks

Primitive shapes, pen/path editing, boolean operations when stable, strokes/fills/gradients, masks, clipping, and reusable visual components.

## 6. Image editing

Crop, rotate, flip, perspective/basic transform, masks, exposure/contrast/saturation/temperature, filters, blur/sharpen, shadow, opacity, blend modes, and non-destructive adjustment stacks.

## 7. Smart object/background edits

Background removal, object selection/removal, inpainting/outpainting, subject isolation, generative fill, layer reconstruction from a flat image, and quality/rights disclosure. Local or remote providers are pluggable.

## 8. Templates, components, brand/style system

Editable templates, reusable components/instances, design tokens, brand kits, style profiles, locked regions, approved assets, and hard/soft rules.

## 9. Smart layout and constraints

Align/distribute, auto layout, grids, spacing systems, text fit, content-aware placement, collision/overflow detection, responsive constraints, and agent-generated layout plans.

## 10. Responsive resize, variants, bulk data, and localisation

Create 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9, banner, thumbnail, and custom variants; bind CSV/JSON/table data; translate/copy-fit; preserve hierarchy; review differences in a grid.

## 11. Multi-track video timeline

Video, audio, caption, overlay, effect, and marker tracks; playhead; snapping; zoom; waveform; linked media; proxies; track lock/visibility/mute/solo.

## 12. Core clip editing

Import/probe, insert, move, split, trim, ripple delete, reorder, duplicate, crop/reframe, reverse, freeze frame, speed/duration, linked/unlinked audio, and precise numeric time editing.

## 13. Keyframes, easing, and motion

Animate position, scale, rotation, opacity, crop/mask, effect parameters, text/graphic properties, audio automation, and camera/framing values. Include presets plus graph/easing editor and interpolation semantics.

## 14. Transitions, effects, blur, and compositing

Crossfade and motion transitions, filters, colour adjustments, background/region blur, masks, blend modes, chroma/subject operations where supported, overlays, motion blur candidates, and effect stacks.

## 15. Audio editing and mixing

Waveforms, gain, mute/solo, fade in/out, crossfades, normalisation, loudness target, voice enhancement, denoise provider, EQ/compression basics later, music ducking, detach/replace audio, and clipping detection.

## 16. Transcripts, captions, and translation

Speech-to-text job, timestamped transcript, caption track, language detection, translation, editable cues, font/style/animation presets, safe areas, line-length/timing checks, SRT/VTT import/export.

## 17. Transcript-based editing

Select transcript spans to remove/reorder media, detect silence and fillers, create highlights/chapters, protect context, preview ripple changes, and keep transcript/timeline mappings versioned.

## 18. Agent planning and smart workflows

Brief-to-plan, asset retrieval, candidate directions, smart edits, clip selection, automatic reframing, version creation, caption/audio pipeline, quality validation, and reusable ChaseOS-neutral recipes.

## 19. Quality validation, export, render, and publish preview

Missing asset/font/effect checks, overflow/contrast/safe-area/accessibility checks, media probe, render presets, PNG/JPEG/WebP/PDF/SVG where supported, MP4/audio/subtitle formats, platform preview, and later exact approved publishing.

## 20. History, versions, comments, review, and approvals

Semantic operation history, snapshots, undo/redo, branches/variants, comments, comparison, approval status, audit/provenance, and recovery. Real-time collaboration is later; local review is foundational.

## 21. Operator analytics, style learning, and coaching

Time/operations saved, common edits, rejection patterns, style profile evidence, quality trends, export corrections, agent/harness reliability, cost, and explainable suggestions that teach better design/video orchestration.

## Priority map

### P0 alpha

1–6, 8–12, basic 15–20, core agent operations.

### P1 beta

7, 13–14, advanced 15–18, full 21, richer imports and style intelligence.

### P2 later

real-time collaboration, broad marketplace, advanced compositing/colour/audio, mobile, web parity, extensive publishing integrations, third-party native effects.
