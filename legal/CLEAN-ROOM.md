# Clean-room product research protocol

## Allowed inputs

- public product documentation;
- public marketing pages;
- public help-centre articles;
- public interoperability documentation;
- independently performed usability observations;
- user-described outcomes and pain points;
- published research and standards;
- legally licensed/open-source dependencies.

## Prohibited inputs

- leaked or reverse-engineered proprietary source;
- private prompts or internal documents;
- copied templates, icons, illustrations, sound packs, fonts, music, or brand copy;
- decompiled code in violation of terms/law;
- screenshots used as a direct pixel-for-pixel implementation target;
- proprietary datasets without rights.

## Documentation requirement

For each major feature, record:

- user outcome being implemented;
- public sources that informed the requirement;
- original semantic model;
- original UI/interaction decisions;
- dependencies and licences;
- contributors/agents involved;
- tests showing independent behaviour.

This file is an engineering protocol and not legal advice.
