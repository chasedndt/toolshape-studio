# Licensing checklist

Before any public release:

- [ ] counsel reviewed product and contributor licensing;
- [ ] project owns or can license every source file;
- [ ] dependency lockfiles and licence scan are current;
- [ ] Apache/MPL/AGPL/other NOTICE obligations are satisfied;
- [ ] FFmpeg configuration and codec obligations are documented;
- [ ] model weights and model licences are tracked separately;
- [ ] training/evaluation datasets have documented rights;
- [ ] fonts, music, stock, templates, effects, and icons have provenance;
- [ ] app-store/platform SDK terms are reviewed;
- [ ] third-party AI provider output/use terms are reviewed;
- [ ] privacy notice and retention claims match the system;
- [ ] trademark/company/domain clearance is complete;
- [ ] x402/payment compliance is reviewed for target jurisdictions;
- [ ] export controls, sanctions, consumer protection, VAT/tax, and refunds are considered.
